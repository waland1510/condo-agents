Art. 1038. Divided co-ownership of an immovable is established by the publication of a declaration under which ownership of the immovable is divided into fractions belonging to one or several persons.

Art. 1039. Upon the publication of the declaration of co-ownership, the co-owners as a body constitute a legal person, the syndicate, whose objects are to preserve the immovable, to maintain and manage the common portions, to protect the rights appurtenant to the immovable or the co-ownership, and to take all measures of common interest.

Art. 1041. The relative value of each fraction is established, at the time the declaration is published, on the basis of the nature, destination, dimensions and location of the private portion of each fraction. It determines the co-owner's share of the common expenses and the number of votes.

Art. 1053. The declaration of co-ownership comprises the act constituting the co-ownership, the by-laws of the immovable and the description of the fractions.

Art. 1057. The by-laws of the immovable may be set up against a lessee or occupant of a private portion from the time a copy of them is given to that person by the co-owner or the syndicate.

Art. 1063. Each co-owner has the disposal of their fraction and free use and enjoyment of their private portion and of the common portions, provided they observe the by-laws of the immovable and do not impair the rights of the other co-owners or the destination of the immovable.

Art. 1064. Each co-owner contributes to the common expenses in proportion to the relative value of their fraction, including the expenses arising from the co-ownership and the operation of the immovable and the contributions to the contingency fund.

Art. 1068.1. Before a co-owner sells a fraction, the syndicate must, on request, give the prospective buyer the documents and information needed to assess the state of the immovable and of the syndicate, including the by-laws, the financial statements, the contingency fund study and the minutes of the general meetings. The syndicate may charge a reasonable fee.

Art. 1070. The syndicate keeps a register of the co-owners and lessees, the minutes of meetings, the by-laws and their amendments, the financial statements, the contingency fund study, the maintenance log and every other document relating to the immovable and the syndicate. A co-owner may consult it.

Art. 1071. The syndicate establishes a contingency fund, liquid and available at short notice, allocated solely to major repairs and to the replacement of common portions, in an amount based on a contingency fund study. The fund is the property of the syndicate. The study must be revised periodically.

Art. 1072. Each year the board of directors fixes the co-owners' contributions to the common expenses after determining the sums required for the expenses of the current year and the contributions to the contingency fund. The co-owners are notified at least once a year of the amount and the dates on which contributions are payable.

Art. 1073. The syndicate has an insurable interest in the whole immovable and must insure it against ordinary risks for its replacement cost. The deductible is a common expense, subject to any reimbursement the by-laws or the law allow the syndicate to claim from a co-owner.

Art. 1074.2. The amount of the deductible provided for in the syndicate's insurance policy is a common expense, but the syndicate may claim from a co-owner the deductible and the cost of repairs that are not covered when damage is caused by that co-owner or by a person for whom the co-owner is responsible.

Art. 1077. The syndicate is liable for damage caused to the co-owners or to third persons by faulty design, construction defects or lack of maintenance of the common portions, without prejudice to any countervailing claim.

Art. 1086. The syndicate may, on application to the court, obtain the resiliation of the lease of a private portion where the lessee's non-performance causes serious injury to a co-owner or to another occupant of the immovable.

Art. 1096. Decisions of the syndicate, including those to amend the declaration of co-ownership, are taken by a majority of the votes of the co-owners present or represented at the meeting, unless the law requires a larger majority.

Art. 1097. Decisions concerning acts of acquisition or alienation of immovables by the syndicate, work for the alteration, enlargement or improvement of the common portions and its apportionment, the construction of buildings to create new fractions, and the amendment of the act constituting the co-ownership or of the description of the fractions require a majority of three-quarters of the votes of all the co-owners.

Art. 1102. Any decision of the syndicate that, contrary to the declaration of co-ownership, imposes on a co-owner a change in the relative value of their fraction, a change of destination of their private portion or a change in the use they may make of it is without effect.

Art. 1103. A co-owner may apply to the court to annul or amend a decision of the general meeting that is biased, taken with intent to injure the co-owners or in contempt of their rights, or that is in error. The application must be brought within sixty days of the meeting.

Art. 1106. The syndicate may be dissolved by a decision of three-quarters of the co-owners representing ninety per cent of the votes of all the co-owners.
