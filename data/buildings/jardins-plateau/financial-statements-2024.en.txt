NOTES TO THE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS (EXTRACT)
Note 4 — Special assessment. By resolution 2023-04, a special assessment of 450,000 $ for roof replacement is payable in three equal instalments due May 1, 2024, 2025 and 2026. Instalments collected to December 31, 2024 total 276,300 $ against 300,000 $ billed.
Note 6 — Insurance. The building policy carries a 25,000 $ deductible for water damage claims and a 10,000 $ deductible for other claims.
Note 7 — Contingent liability. A claim of 85,000 $ was served on the syndicate in March 2025 by the owner of a unit on the fourth floor for water infiltration. The outcome cannot be determined at the date of these statements. No provision has been recorded.
Note 8 — Monthly common expenses. The 2025 budget sets monthly contributions between 348 $ and 612 $ per unit depending on relative value; a 2.083 % fraction pays 412 $ per month, of which 41 $ is the contingency fund contribution.
